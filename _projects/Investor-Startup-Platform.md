Built for the 2022 hackathon run by Silicon Valley Bank and Arizona State University, on the premise that the hard part of early-stage funding is not the money but the matching: investors and founders looking for each other with no shared vocabulary for what either one wants.

The application let both sides describe themselves in structured terms and surfaced plausible matches from that, wrapped in a full-stack web app built over the course of the weekend.
