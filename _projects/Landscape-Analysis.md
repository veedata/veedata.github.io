Publicly available data on internet (Surface web /Deep web) is an efficient source of information to understand the Online Child Sexual Abuse (OCSA) and Online Child Sexual Exploitation (OCSE). The Landscape report presented is intended to give a snapshot or overview of the reported incidences and the different form that have evolved in OCSA and OCSE as the penetration of the online technology has amplified.

This project was funded a grant of <b>USD 91,666.80</b> by Children's Investment Fund Foundation (CIFF) in the UK.
