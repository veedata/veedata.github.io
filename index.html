Ph.D. Student · Arizona State University

Viraj Thakkar

I teach databases new tricks and poke at storage engines until they spill their secrets.

Hi, 👋 I’m a perpetually-curious CS Ph.D. student who gets way too excited about LSM-based key-value stores, disaggregated infrastructure, and storage systems for AI/ML. My favorite thing is taking a big, intimidating system apart just to see what makes it tick (sometimes literally), and then, on a good day, putting it back together a little bit better than I found it.
