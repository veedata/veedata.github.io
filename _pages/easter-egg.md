Oh! Hi. You weren't supposed to find this — which means you went poking around
the source, or someone whispered the secret to you, or you're just the kind of
wonderfully nosy person who tries `/there-be-dragons/` on a whim. All three are
excellent qualities.

There's no sitemap breadcrumb leading here. No robot has ever read this page.
It's just you, me, and the quiet hum of a server that has no idea how special
this moment is.

So while we're here, a confession: I think the most fun part of computer science
is the *taking-apart*. The gleeful, slightly-reckless disassembly of a system
that everyone insists is too complicated to understand. Spoiler — it usually
isn't. It's just a very tall stack of small, knowable things, and if you're
patient (and caffeinated) you can climb the whole thing.

That's the whole trick. Stay curious, keep climbing, and be nice to the dragons
you meet along the way. 🐲
